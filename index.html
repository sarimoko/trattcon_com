Major Red Flags to Watch For

Before signing a contract or handing over funds for roofing or home repairs, be aware of these common signs of fraudulent operations:

  • No Verifiable License: Refusal or inability to provide a current, state-issued contractor’s license number.
  • High Upfront Demands: Pressuring property owners for large cash deposits or full payment before any materials arrive or work begins.
  • Lack of Written Contracts: Relying strictly on verbal agreements, or using vague, non-binding paperwork that lacks official business details.
  • Unsolicited Door-to-Door Solicitations: Traveling "crews" offering immediate roofing repairs using leftover materials from a previous job.
  • Unprofessional Conduct: Erratic behavior on the job site, safety violations, or signs of substance impairment while operating machinery.

How to Verify a Contractor Legally

Take the following steps to ensure your project is completed safely, legally, and professionally:

  1. Check the State License Board: Always look up the contractor’s license status on your official state or local regulatory website to confirm it is active and in good standing.
  2. Request Proof of Insurance: Verify that the business carries general liability insurance and workers' compensation to protect your property from liability.
  3. Obtain Multiple Written Estimates: Do not be pressured by "limited-time" offers. Legitimate businesses provide detailed, written bids that break down labor and material costs.
  4. Report Fraudulent Activity: If you suspect scams, unlicensed activity, or dangerous behavior on a job site, contact local law enforcement or your state’s Department of Consumer Affairs immediately.
